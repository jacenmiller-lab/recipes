# Six-Minute Seared Ahi Tuna Steaks

## Instructions

1. Pat the tuna steaks dry. If they were frozen, make sure they're fully thawed.
2. Whisk the coconut aminos, sesame oil, coconut sugar, salt and pepper (and cayenne, if using) until the sugar dissolves.
3. Coat the steaks in the marinade. Marinating is optional; 10 minutes is plenty, or up to overnight in the fridge. Save a spoonful of marinade for drizzling if you like.
4. Heat a cast iron skillet over medium-high to high heat until very hot, 3–5 minutes. For nonstick, use medium to medium-high and about 1 minute.
5. Add the oil, then sear the steaks about 1–1½ minutes per side for medium-rare. Don't crowd the pan; sear in batches if all 4 don't fit.
6. Move to a cutting board, rest briefly, then slice about ½" thick. Garnish with green onions, sesame seeds and lime.

## Notes

- **Doneness:** about 30 seconds per side for very rare, 2–2½ minutes per side for medium-well. Thinner steaks need only about a minute per side.
- Slice soon after searing. The longer it rests, the more it keeps cooking.
- Coconut sugar scorches like honey does, so turn the heat down a little if the marinade starts to burn.
- Coconut aminos are less salty than soy sauce, so keep the ½ tsp salt.
