# Pistachio Crusted Coriander Salmon

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Pulse the pistachios in a food processor or Vitamix until mostly fine, with some coarser bits left for texture. Tip them into a bowl.
3. Add the celery salt, cardamom, coriander, onion powder, pepper and 2 tbsp olive oil, and mix into a paste.
4. Salt the salmon fillets, then press the pistachio paste over the tops.
5. Oil a cast iron skillet or baking pan and set the fillets in skin side down.
6. Bake up to 30 minutes, checking every 10 minutes. If the crust starts to get too dark, lay a sheet of foil loosely over the pan for the rest of the time.
7. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes

- Bake time depends on how thick the fillets are. Thinner fillets can be done in under 20 minutes, so start checking early. The salmon is done when it flakes easily or reads about 130–140°F in the thickest part (the USDA recommends 145°F).
- The pistachio paste covers 2–4 fillets, so it's fine to make the full amount even for fewer fillets.
