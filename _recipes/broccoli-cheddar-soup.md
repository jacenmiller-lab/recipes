# Broccoli Cheddar Soup

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Cook the onion until soft, 7–9 minutes.
2. Add the broccoli, toss with the onion, and cook 2–3 minutes.
3. Stir in the white wine vinegar.
4. Add the chicken broth, then the onion powder and garlic powder. Season generously with salt and pepper.
5. Whisk in the arrowroot powder, 1 tablespoon at a time, until fully mixed in.
6. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer 8–10 minutes, until the broccoli is tender.
7. Stir in the cheddar until melted.
8. Taste and season again with salt and pepper. Serve topped with extra cheddar.

## Notes

- Serves 6–8.
- **Smoother thickening:** arrowroot can clump when it hits hot liquid. Whisking it into a few tablespoons of cold broth or water first, then stirring that in, avoids lumps.
- **Lower-carb option:** about ½ tsp xanthan gum can replace the arrowroot, sprinkled in slowly while whisking.
- Grating your own cheddar melts smoother than pre-shredded, which has anti-caking starch.
- Turn the heat to low before adding the cheese so it doesn't turn grainy.
