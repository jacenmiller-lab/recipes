# Instant Pot Chicken Paprikash Stew

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken thighs generously with salt and pepper.
2. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté and heat the olive oil until it shimmers. Cook the pepper and onion until soft, 3–4 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and cook 1–2 minutes more.
4. Stir in the paprika, smoked paprika and onion powder.
5. Add the chicken thighs skin side down and let them brown for 3–4 minutes.
6. Stir in the diced tomatoes, scraping up anything stuck to the bottom of the pot.
7. Put the lid on, set the vent to Sealing, and cook on Manual (high pressure) for 20 minutes.
8. Let the pressure release naturally, until the float valve drops, before opening the lid.
9. Move the chicken to a plate and cover it with foil.
10. Switch back to Sauté, bring the sauce to a boil, and let it reduce for 8–10 minutes.
11. Plate the chicken, spoon the sauce over it, and top with sour cream if you like.

## Notes

- Serves 4–6.
- Scraping the bottom of the pot after adding the tomatoes helps prevent the Instant Pot's "Burn" warning.
- The pressure time stays at 20 minutes even if you scale up, as long as it fits under the pot's max fill line.
