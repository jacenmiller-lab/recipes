# Crock-Pot Breakfast Casserole

## Instructions

1. Spray the slow cooker and spread the hash browns evenly across the bottom.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs well. Whisk in the milk, ground mustard, and plenty of salt and fresh pepper. Set aside.
3. Brown the sausage over high heat, breaking it up, then drain.
4. Spread the sausage over the hash browns, then add the cheese (the whole bag) and mix it all together.
5. Pour the egg mixture over everything and use a wooden spoon to spread it out evenly.
6. Cook on low for 6–8 hours.

## Notes

- **Add-ins:** before cooking, you can mix in chunks of sourdough bread, diced green chiles, salsa or diced green onions.
- Ground mustard may sound like an odd addition, but the original author swore by it.
