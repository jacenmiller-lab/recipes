# Philly Cheesesteaks

## Instructions

### Prep the steak

1. Put the steak in the freezer for 2–3 hours, until firm but not frozen solid.
2. Slice it as thin as you can, trimming out any hard bits of gristle. Season the slices well with salt and pepper.

### Peppers and onions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large flat-bottomed sauté pan over medium-high heat until it shimmers.
2. Add the onions and peppers and toss to coat in the oil.
3. Stir in the onion powder and garlic powder and season with salt and pepper.
4. Cover, turn the heat to low, and simmer 15–20 minutes, until soft.

### Steak and cheese

1. Heat a large nonstick griddle or sauté pan over medium-high heat. Cook the steak, flipping it with a spatula, and season once with salt and pepper as it cooks.
2. Add the peppers and onions, toss with the steak, and season once more.
3. Divide the mixture into portions in the pan and lay a slice of cheese over each one to melt.
4. Lift each portion out and serve on a salad or a plate, topped with banana peppers, tomato, mayo and mustard as you like.

## Notes

- Partly freezing the steak is what makes it easy to slice paper-thin, so don't skip it.
