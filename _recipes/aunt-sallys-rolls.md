# Aunt Sally's Refrigerator Rolls

*"Sans refrigeration," as Aunt Sally put it. Her note on the card: "Sorry it's so slow!"*

## Instructions

### Milk mixture

1. In a saucepan on the stove, heat the milk, ½ cup sugar, salt and oil until steaming. Don't let it boil.
2. Let it cool until lukewarm.

### Yeast

1. While the milk cools, dissolve the yeast in the ¼ cup lukewarm water with 1 tsp sugar. Let it sit and get foamy.

### Dough

1. Once the milk mixture is cool, beat in the eggs.
2. Sift the flour if you like (Aunt Sally says sifting is optional).
3. Stir the yeast mixture into the milk and egg mixture, then add half the flour. Mix well, cover, and let rise 1 hour.
4. Knead in the rest of the flour, then let rise 1 more hour. If the dough seems dry, hold back some of the flour. Aunt Sally sometimes did.
5. Knead again and divide into 4 equal parts. Roll each one out and cut into rolls.
6. Top with sesame seeds if you like, then let the rolls rise 1 more hour.
7. Bake at 350°F for about 15–20 minutes, until golden.

## Notes

- Makes about 5 dozen rolls. Each quarter of the dough makes about 15.
- **Yeast cakes:** the card calls for fresh (compressed) yeast cakes, which can be hard to find now. One 0.6 oz cake equals one ¼ oz packet (2¼ tsp) of active dry yeast, so use 2 packets (4½ tsp) for a full batch.
- Lukewarm means about 105–110°F. Hotter than that can kill the yeast.
