# Keto Chili

## Instructions

### Start (all methods)

1. Heat a little olive oil over medium-high heat (in a skillet, or the Instant Pot on Sauté, or a Dutch oven for stovetop). Cook the onion 5–7 minutes, until translucent.
2. Add the garlic and cook about 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. Add the ground beef and brown it, 8–10 minutes, breaking it apart as it cooks. Drain the fat if you're using fattier beef or it looks greasy.

### Slow cooker

1. Move the beef mixture to the slow cooker.
2. Stir in everything else except the bay leaf and the beef broth. Tuck the bay leaf into the middle.
3. Cook on low 6–8 hours or high 3–4 hours. Remove the bay leaf before serving.

### Instant Pot

1. Leave the beef mixture in the pot. Stir in everything else, including the 1 cup beef broth, except the bay leaf. Tuck the bay leaf into the middle.
2. Press Cancel, close the lid, and cook on Meat/Stew for 35 minutes.
3. Let the pressure release naturally if you have time, or quick release. Remove the bay leaf before serving.

### Stovetop

1. In the Dutch oven, stir in everything else except the bay leaf and the beef broth. Tuck the bay leaf into the middle.
2. Cover and simmer about 1 hour, stirring now and then. Remove the bay leaf before serving.

## Notes

- **Don't skip browning** the onion, garlic and beef; it makes a big difference in flavor.
- This is a thick, chunky chili. For thinner chili, add a little water or broth before cooking. The Instant Pot version comes out thinner because of the added broth.
- Yes, it really is ¼ cup chili powder.
- Keeps 3–5 days in the fridge and freezes well.
