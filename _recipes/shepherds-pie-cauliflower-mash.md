# Shepherd's Pie with Cauliflower Mash

## Instructions

### Filling

1. Over medium-high heat, cook the mushrooms in 1 tbsp olive oil until well browned. Set aside.
2. In the same pan, cook the onion in the other 1 tbsp olive oil until soft, about 8 minutes.
3. Add the carrots, garlic, parsley, onion salt, garlic salt, thyme and oregano. Cook another 5–10 minutes, until soft, then set aside with the mushrooms.
4. Brown the ground beef and pork, breaking it up as it cooks, until cooked through. Drain the fat and return the meat to the pan.
5. Stir in the chicken bouillon, then the mushroom and onion mixture. Taste and add salt and pepper if needed.

### Cauliflower mash

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Boil the cauliflower in a large pot of water for 8–11 minutes, until fork-tender.
3. Drain well and press out as much water as you can with paper towels.
4. Blend the cauliflower, cream cheese and heavy cream in a blender or food processor until it's like mashed potatoes. Salt it partway through, and add a splash more cream if it's too thick.

### Assemble and bake

1. Spread the meat mixture in a deep casserole dish.
2. Spread the cauliflower mash over the top, then sprinkle with a thin layer of parmesan.
3. Bake 30–40 minutes, until the parmesan is lightly golden.

## Notes

- Serves 4–6.
- Getting the cauliflower as dry as possible keeps the mash from turning watery.
