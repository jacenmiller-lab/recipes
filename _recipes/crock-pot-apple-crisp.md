# Crock-Pot Apple Crisp

## Instructions

### Apple filling

1. Put the chopped apples in the slow cooker. Add the lemon juice and apple butter and toss to coat.
2. In a small bowl, mix the xylitol or allulose, xanthan gum, and 1 tbsp apple pie spice. Sprinkle it evenly over the apples and stir well so the xanthan gum doesn't clump.
3. Scatter the walnuts over the apples and stir them in.

### Crisp topping

1. In a bowl, combine the almond flour, coconut sugar, oats, the other 1 tbsp apple pie spice, and the salt.
2. Cut in the cold butter with a pastry cutter or fork until the mixture is crumbly.

### Cook: topping in the slow cooker

1. Spread the topping evenly over the apples.
2. Lay 2–3 layers of paper towels (or a clean, thin kitchen towel) across the top of the slow cooker, then set the lid on top. Fold any overhang up onto the lid so it stays away from the heating base.
3. Cook on low for about 4 hours, until the apples are tender. If the towels get soaked partway through, swap in fresh ones.
4. For the last 30–45 minutes, remove the towels and prop the lid open a little with a wooden spoon so steam can escape and the topping dries out.
5. Serve warm, with ice cream if you like.

### Option: oven-crisped topping (crispiest)

1. Cook the apple filling alone in the slow cooker, covered with the towels and lid, on low for about 4 hours, until tender.
2. About 30 minutes before the apples are done, heat the oven to 325°F and line a sheet pan with parchment.
3. Spread the topping on the pan in an even layer and bake 12–18 minutes, stirring halfway, until golden. Watch closely near the end, because almond flour browns fast.
4. Let it cool on the pan for a few minutes. It crisps up as it cools.
5. Spoon the apples into bowls and sprinkle the topping over each one just before serving. Store leftover topping separately so it stays crunchy.

## Notes

- **Keeping the topping crisp:** a slow cooker traps steam, which drips back down and softens the topping. The towels catch that condensation, and venting at the end lets the top dry out. For the crispiest topping, use the oven option above.
- **Xylitol and dogs:** xylitol is highly toxic to dogs, even in small amounts. If you use it, keep leftovers and dishes out of the dog's reach. Allulose is the dog-safe option.
- **Sweetness:** allulose is about 70% as sweet as sugar, so the filling will be slightly less sweet with it than with xylitol.
- **Lower-carb swaps:** the original recipe uses ¾ cup all-purpose flour in the topping and 1 tbsp cornstarch in the filling. Almond flour and xanthan gum replace them here.
- Six Granny Smith apples is a little more than the original 6 cups. The topping still covers it fine.
