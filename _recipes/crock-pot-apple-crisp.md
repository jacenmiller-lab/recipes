# Crock-Pot Apple Crisp

## Instructions

### Apple filling

1. Put the chopped apples in the slow cooker. Add the lemon juice and apple butter and toss to coat.
2. In a small bowl, mix the xylitol or allulose, cornstarch, and 1 tbsp apple pie spice. Pour it over the apples and stir.
3. Scatter the walnuts over the apples and stir them in.

### Crisp topping

1. In a bowl, combine the flour, coconut sugar, oats, the other 1 tbsp apple pie spice, and the salt.
2. Cut in the cold butter with a pastry cutter or fork until the mixture is crumbly.
3. Spread the topping evenly over the apples.

### Cook

1. Lay 2–3 layers of paper towels (or a clean, thin kitchen towel) across the top of the slow cooker, then set the lid on top. Fold any overhang up onto the lid so it stays away from the heating base.
2. Cook on low for about 4 hours, until the apples are tender. If the towels get soaked partway through, swap in fresh ones.
3. For the last 30–45 minutes, remove the towels and prop the lid open a little with a wooden spoon so steam can escape and the topping dries out.
4. Serve warm, with ice cream if you like.

## Notes

- **Keeping the topping crisp:** a slow cooker traps steam, which drips back down and softens the topping. The towels catch that condensation, and venting at the end lets the top dry out. For the crispiest result, bake the topping separately on a parchment-lined sheet pan at 350°F for about 15–20 minutes, stirring once, and spoon it over the apples just before serving.
- **Xylitol and dogs:** xylitol is highly toxic to dogs, even in small amounts. If you use it, keep leftovers and dishes out of the dog's reach. Allulose is the dog-safe option.
- **Sweetness:** allulose is about 70% as sweet as sugar, so the filling will be slightly less sweet with it than with xylitol.
- **Lower-carb swaps:** almond flour can stand in for the all-purpose flour in the topping, and a pinch (about ¼ tsp) of xanthan gum can replace the cornstarch in the filling.
- Six Granny Smith apples is a little more than the original 6 cups. The topping still covers it fine.
