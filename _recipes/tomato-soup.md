# Tomato Soup

## Instructions

1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic.
2. In a large pot, melt the butter over medium heat.
3. Add the onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
5. Stir in the tomato puree, diced tomatoes, and tomato paste.
6. Add the chicken broth, using more or less for the thickness you like.
7. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
9. Turn the heat to low, stir in the heavy cream, and heat through for 2–3 minutes.
10. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth.

## Notes

- If you can't find a 28 oz can of diced tomatoes, use two 14.5 oz cans.
- Use vegetable broth to make it vegetarian.
